AI settings

JobMatch AI talks to your own backend server. The AI API key lives only in that server's .env file, never in this extension.

Use http://localhost:3000 for local development. Remote servers must use https:// and Chrome will ask for permission.

Privacy

  • Only when you ask. The extension reads a page only after you click Analyze or Select Job Text, or right-click selected text. It never runs in the background on sites you browse.
  • What is sent for analysis: the job text (or the part you selected) plus the profile fields needed to compare: skills, languages, frameworks, tools, education, experience, projects, preferences and resume text. Your name, links and salary expectation are not sent.
  • For resume and cover letter drafts your name and profile links are also included so the draft can have a header.
  • Where it goes: to your JobMatch AI backend, which forwards it to the AI provider configured on that server. Other page content, cookies and browsing history are never sent.
  • Retention: the backend does not store or log requests. The AI provider applies its own data policy, so review it for the provider you use.
  • Local storage: your profile, saved jobs and applications stay in this browser (chrome.storage.local). It is not encrypted, so anyone with access to your browser profile could read it. Delete it any time below.
  • No selling, no analytics, no auto-apply. JobMatch AI never submits an application for you.

Data management

Saved jobs are the stored postings and their analyses. Applications are your tracker entries.
